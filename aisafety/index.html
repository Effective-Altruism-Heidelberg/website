4.11. SAIGE Talk: The Why and How of AI Compute Verification

We, 18:00 - meetup place TBD
Join us in watching and discussing Safe AI Germany’s online talk on AI Compute Verification!

Full details on Luma: https://luma.com/ydmm1561


While verification of AI agreements is still emerging, it is already established practice in other high-stakes scenarios. Examples include: Nuclear inspectors monitor fissile material, and chemical weapons treaties come with on-site inspections.

For AI, comparable verification tools are only now being developed.

Yannick will talk about:

  • why states and AI companies are interested in technical options for verifying AI agreements,

  • what verification could look like in practice, and what lessons carry over from nuclear and chemical governance, and

  • where the field stands today and what to expect next.
